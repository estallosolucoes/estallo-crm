# Estallo CRM

Estallo's hosted SaaS for WhatsApp sales and customer service. The product uses a fixed Estallo identity and separate organization accounts.

Plans currently shown are illustrative: they do not charge customers or enforce quotas. Stripe integration will use Nutrigg as the reference; credentials and final prices are pending.

The canonical product documentation is maintained in Portuguese: [Product vision](VISION.md), [Current direction](docs/estallo-produto.md), [Development](README.md), [Operations](docs/estallo-vps.md).
