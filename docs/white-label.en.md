# Estallo CRM identity

The commercial product has a fixed Estallo brand. The inherited white-label configuration does not define the look of the SaaS. See [the current direction](estallo-produto.md).

The client company keeps its registration data and its identity in commercial documents. The interface, titles, icons, access and product outputs keep the Estallo identity.

Legacy schemas and contracts are preserved at this stage, with no data removal or renaming migrations.
